An Interactive Image Guessing Game

Guess the Image helps students practice vocabulary by gradually revealing a picture. Choose lesson items, select a reveal mode, and let students identify the image before all of its sections are uncovered.

How to Play

  1. Choose a category, language, and tags.
  2. Use random items or select specific images.
  3. Choose manual or automatic image reveal.
  4. Reveal the image and select the correct answer.

Two Reveal Modes

Manual mode lets the teacher or students uncover sections of the picture. Automatic mode reveals the picture gradually for a timed guessing challenge.

Classroom Activity Ideas

  • Vocabulary introduction and review
  • Speaking and pronunciation practice
  • Image recognition and memory activities
  • Whole-class or team guessing games

Playing as a Class or Independently

Young learners can play individually or in teams. In manual mode, either the teacher or a student can uncover the picture one section at a time. Automatic mode is useful for independent play or whenever the class wants a steadily timed reveal.

Encouraging Thoughtful Guesses

Students may say an answer aloud before selecting a choice, but this is optional. In a whole-class game, ask learners to raise their hands or take turns so that everyone has an opportunity to think and respond.

Building Difficulty

Begin with familiar vocabulary when introducing a lesson. Later, use more difficult words or similar-looking pictures to check recognition, recall, and careful observation.

Guess the Image is designed especially for young learners. It can introduce new vocabulary and lesson topics, support speaking practice, or provide a quick review activity.
