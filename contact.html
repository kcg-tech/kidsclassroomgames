What You Can Contact Us About

  • Problems with a classroom game or printable tool
  • Suggestions for new resources, images, or vocabulary
  • Questions about accounts or saved materials
  • Privacy, student-data, or deletion requests
  • Copyright or content concerns

When reporting a technical problem, please include the page name, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. Do not send student names, classroom records, passwords, or other sensitive information.
