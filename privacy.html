Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026

Kids Classroom Games provides classroom activities, printable teacher tools, and interactive games. This Privacy Policy explains what information we process, why we process it, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Process

Registered teacher and adult accounts

When an adult creates an account, we may process:

  • Email address and account identifier
  • Account creation and login information
  • Saved game boards, Bingo sets, and preferences
  • Account type and access status

Multiplayer Bingo participants

Students do not need an account or email address to join a Bingo room. During a game, we temporarily process:

  • The nickname entered by the participant
  • An anonymous technical identifier
  • Room membership and readiness status
  • Bingo-card selections and marked squares
  • Wins and related game timestamps

Participants should use a nickname and should not enter a full name or other personal information.

Technical information

Our hosting, database, security, and email providers may automatically process limited technical information such as IP addresses, browser or device information, request timestamps, and security or error logs.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Save teacher-created classroom resources
  • Operate multiplayer rooms and game features
  • Send confirmation and account-related emails
  • Protect the website from misuse and security threats
  • Diagnose errors and improve website performance
  • Respond to support and privacy requests

We do not sell personal information.

3. Children and Student Privacy

Kids Classroom Games is designed primarily for use by teachers and for teacher-led classroom activities. Students are not asked to create accounts or provide email addresses.

Multiplayer participants should use classroom nicknames rather than full names. Teachers should not ask students to enter personal information into game fields.

If we learn that a child has provided unnecessary personal information, a parent, guardian, or school may contact us to request its review or deletion.

4. Retention and Deletion

Temporary multiplayer Bingo data is designed to be automatically removed approximately 24 hours after a game session ends or expires.

Registered account information and saved teacher resources are retained while the account remains active or until deletion is requested, except where limited retention is necessary for security, legal, or operational reasons.

Infrastructure and security logs may remain for limited periods according to our service providers’ retention practices.

5. Service Providers

We use trusted service providers to operate the website. These may include:

  • Supabase for authentication, database, and live-game services
  • Cloudflare for domain, security, hosting, and email forwarding
  • Resend for account-related email delivery
  • GitHub for source-code management and beta deployment

These providers process information only as needed to provide their services, maintain security, and comply with applicable law.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

The website may use browser storage and authentication tokens to keep users signed in, remember settings, and support game functionality.

Advertising services are not currently enabled. This Privacy Policy will be updated before advertising or advertising cookies are introduced.

7. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent user information. Information may be disclosed:

  • To service providers that operate the website
  • When required by law or a valid legal request
  • To investigate fraud, abuse, or security threats
  • To protect users, the public, or the website

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information.

Registered users may delete saved resources or permanently delete their account through the My Account page. Users may also contact us using the address below for other privacy requests.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

10. International Processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than the user’s country. We take reasonable steps to use providers that maintain appropriate privacy and security protections.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the website changes. The effective date at the top will be revised when material changes are made.

12. Contact Us

For privacy questions, account deletion, or other requests:

support@kidsclassroomgames.com
