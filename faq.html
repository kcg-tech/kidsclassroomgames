Help and Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about using the classroom tools, saving resources, running live games, and using generated materials with learners.

Using Kids Classroom Games

Who is Kids Classroom Games for?

Kids Classroom Games is designed for teachers, tutors, freelance educators, parents, guardians, and other adults who prepare or supervise learning activities.

Is Kids Classroom Games free to use?

Yes. Kids Classroom Games is currently free to use. Some account features, including the number of saved sets or quizzes, have limits. Advertisements on educator-facing pages help support the website and keep its classroom tools available without a subscription. Student join and gameplay pages do not display advertisements.

Do I need an account?

Many activities can be explored without an account. An adult account is required for features that save sets, quizzes, or other resources. Current saving limits are shown within each supported tool and may change as the website develops.

Which languages and subjects does the website support?

The image library and language options support English, Japanese, and ESL learning, but the website is not limited to language lessons. Educators can use number resources for mathematics, body-part images for science, and customizable games for general knowledge or almost any subject they teach.

Classroom Materials and Images

Can I use the materials in paid lessons or tutoring sessions?

Yes. Teachers, tutors, freelance educators, parents, and guardians may use materials generated through Kids Classroom Games in classroom lessons, tutoring sessions, and home education, including paid teaching. The image library, downloadable files, and generated materials may not be resold, republished as a competing resource collection, or redistributed as standalone files.

How are the library images created?

Many illustrations in the Kids Classroom Games library were created with generative AI tools. They are selected, reviewed, organized, and sometimes edited by the site creator for use in educational activities.

Can I sell or upload the image library elsewhere?

No. The library and its image files may not be resold, republished, scraped, or distributed as a separate collection. Generated classroom materials may be used in your own teaching under the permissions explained in the Terms of Use.

Live Games and Student Access

Do students need accounts?

No. Students join supported live games using a room code and a classroom nickname. They should not enter a full name, email address, or other personal information.

Are advertisements shown on student pages?

No. Dedicated student join pages and live student gameplay pages do not display advertisements. Advertising may appear on educator-facing setup, resource, and information pages.

What information do live games use?

Live games temporarily use the participant's nickname, anonymous technical identifier, room membership, answers, and related gameplay activity. See the Privacy Policy for more information.

Troubleshooting and Support

What should I do if a tool or live game does not work?

Refresh the page, check the room code or selected settings, and try again. If the problem continues, contact support with the page name, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. Do not send passwords or student information.

How can I suggest a resource or report a problem?

Email support@kidsclassroomgames.com . Suggestions for classroom images, vocabulary, games, and teacher tools are welcome.
