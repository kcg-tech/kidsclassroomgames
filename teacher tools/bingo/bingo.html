Create customized classroom bingo cards using words, pictures, or both. Choose vocabulary that matches your lesson, generate different cards for students, and download a ready-to-print PDF.

How to Create a Bingo Activity

  1. Choose a category, language, and optional tags.
  2. Use all matching items or select specific items.
  3. Choose a 3 × 3 or 4 × 4 bingo grid.
  4. Generate the cards and download the PDF.
