How It Works

Many tools can be tried without an account. An adult account is needed when you want to save reusable sets or quizzes.

  • Teacher Tools: create printable classroom materials.
  • Fun Games: lead customizable activities with the class.
  • Live Games: let students join from their own devices.
  • Random Pickers: make classroom selections more engaging.
  1. Choose a resource.
  2. Select library content or add your own.
  3. Customize it for the lesson.
  4. Print, present, or play with the class.

Educators can adapt the resources to any subject. How they are used is up to the teacher's imagination and the needs of the class.
